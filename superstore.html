SUPERSTORE SALES & PROFIT ANALYSIS

Turning transactional data into actionable business insights.

Advanced Excel Power Query Power Pivot

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to analyze the sales and profitability of the Central Superstore dataset. By transforming raw transactional data into an interactive dashboard, this project uncovers revenue trends, identifies top-performing regions, and segments customers to drive strategic decision-making.

Data Preparation (Power Query)

Raw data is rarely ready for analysis. I used Power Query to ensure data accuracy and consistency:

  • Standardization: Used the Trim function to remove leading and trailing whitespaces from category names.
  • Handling Nulls: Identified missing Postal Code values and replaced them accurately (e.g., mapped nulls to Burlington, VT '05401').
  • Data Integrity: Removed duplicate records and blank rows to prevent skewed metrics.
  • Custom Columns: Created calculated columns such as Profit Margin = [Profit] / [Sales] and applied conditional logic for Sales Category (VIP, High, Medium, Low) based on statistical distribution.

Data Modeling (Power Pivot)

To enable multi-dimensional analysis, I integrated multiple tables using Power Pivot. I established One-to-Many relationships connecting the main Orders fact table with the People and Returns dimension tables, ensuring accurate filtering across the dashboard.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

  • Total Orders: 9,994
  • Total Sales: $2,297,201
  • Total Profit: $286,397
  • Profit Margin: 12.47%
  • Top Region: West ($725K Sales)

Interactive Dashboard

The dashboard provides a high-level view of business health, featuring monthly sales trends, profit by category (Donut Chart), regional sales (Bar Chart), and customer segmentation analysis.

Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

  • The Furniture Profitability Dilemma: While Furniture generates substantial sales ($742K), it yields only $18,451 in profit (~6.4% of total profit) compared to Technology ($145,455) and Office Supplies ($122,491).
    Strategic Action: Restructure vendor pricing agreements and audit shipping costs on bulky furniture items to protect margins without hurting sales volume.
  • Regional Performance & Margin Gaps: The West region dominates both revenue and profitability ($725,458 Sales, $108,418 Profit, 15% Margin). In contrast, the Central region shows the lowest margin (8%) despite generating $501,240 in sales.
    Strategic Action: Replicate the West region’s promotional strategy across other territories while revising discounting limits in the Central region.
  • Q4 Seasonal Spikes: Monthly sales trend analysis shows massive revenue surges starting in September ($307K) and peaking in November ($352K), indicating high holiday seasonal demand compared to a quiet February ($59K).
    Strategic Action: Align supply chain and inventory procurement by August to eliminate stockouts during the September–December peak window.
  • Segment Contribution: The Consumer segment is the core revenue driver ($1,161,401 Sales), outperforming Corporate ($706,146) and Home Office ($429,653).
    Strategic Action: Introduce a loyalty rewards program for top consumer buyers to maximize Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
