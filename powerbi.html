CENTRAL SUPERSTORE BI DASHBOARD

Transforming data into visual stories for strategic growth.

Power BI Power Query DAX Data Modeling

Project Overview

This project leverages Power BI to analyze the performance of the Central Superstore. By designing a comprehensive and interactive dashboard, the goal was to provide real-time insights into sales trends, customer segmentation, and product profitability.

Data Transformation (Power Query)

To ensure high data quality before analysis, I utilized Power Query for rigorous data cleaning:

  • Dimensional Reduction: Removed unnecessary columns to optimize the dataset size and improve performance.
  • Data Integrity: Filtered out blank rows and removed duplicate records to maintain absolute accuracy.
  • Formatting: Standardized data types across all columns for seamless DAX calculations.

Custom Metrics (DAX)

Beyond standard aggregations, I created calculated measures using DAX to extract deeper business insights. For example, I developed a dynamic measure to calculate the Average Order Value (AOV):

Average order value = SUM('Central_Region'[sales]) / DISTINCTCOUNT('Central_Region'[Order ID])

Key Performance Indicators

  • Total Sales: 501.24K

  • Total Orders: 2.323K
  • Average Order Value: 426.59

Interactive Dashboard

The final deliverable is a Dark-Themed, fully interactive dashboard featuring timeline slicers (2013-2017), dynamic tooltips, and cross-filtering across categories, segments, and top customers.

Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

  • Severe Sub-Category Profit Drain: The Sub-Category profit analysis clearly reveals that while Copiers and Phones drive strong positive profits, Tables and Furnishings are operating at a net loss (negative profit bars).
    Strategic Action: Eliminate unprofitable table models or place a minimum order threshold/freight charge on items eroding overall profitability.
  • Technology Category Dominance: Technology accounts for the largest share of overall profit ($33.7K / 79.14%), while Furniture only contributes roughly 20.8% and Office Supplies minimal returns.
    Strategic Action: Prioritize bundle promotions that pair high-margin Technology accessories with standard office supplies.
  • Client Concentration (Pareto Principle): The top 5 customers (led by Tamara Chand with nearly $20K in sales) represent a vital revenue cohort within the region.
    Strategic Action: Assign dedicated corporate account management to the top 10% customer accounts to secure long-term contractual renewals.
  • End-of-Year Revenue Surge: Monthly transaction volume confirms a steady ramp-up from August into sharp spikes across October, November, and December.
    Strategic Action: Plan targeted regional discount campaigns in early Q3 to capture early-bird corporate purchasing budgets.
